<Separation decision rubric (use exactly one label)>
Use these checks in order. "Medoid-consistent" means each boundary side broadly
matches its own cluster medoid's behavior+outcome story.

1) `over_fine` (merge signal):
- both sides are medoid-consistent, AND
- boundary pair behavior is effectively the same (same resolution/motive family), AND
- outcomes are the same (both collision or both safe/near-miss), with no persistent
  geometry split beyond tiny late clearance differences.

2) `justified` (keep split):
- both sides are medoid-consistent, AND at least one is true:
  a) behavior differs across sides (different resolution class or motive family),
     regardless of whether outcomes match, OR
  b) behavior looks similar but outcomes differ, and you can point to a real
     persistent geometric/clearance divergence tied to the outcome (even if subtle).

3) `inconclusive` (insufficient/conflicted evidence):
- either side is NOT medoid-consistent, OR
- evidence is contradictory/missing, OR
- only a tiny late difference is visible and you cannot tie it to a persistent
  divergence confidently.

Do not invent new labels. If you suspect finer sub-structure, keep
`separation_call` within the 3 labels and state the split/subdivide concern in
`separation_reason` and `hypothesis`.
</Separation decision rubric>
