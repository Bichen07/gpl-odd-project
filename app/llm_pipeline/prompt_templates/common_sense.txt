<Domain Rules & Glossary>

**Conflict metrics (authoritative when provided — do not recompute)**
- **Center-to-center distance** (legacy JSON key: `d`): straight-line distance
  between the geometric centers of Ego and the named conflict vehicle (m).
  In pack-level conflict_metrics, this is the closest-approach value for the trial;
  in timeline rows it is the value at that stamp.
- **Ego speed / named vehicle speed** (legacy JSON: `v_ego` / `v_vehicle`): absolute
  ground speeds (m/s), not relative speed.
- **Estimated time to collision** (legacy JSON: `ttc`): center-to-center distance
  divided by its current closing rate while the vehicles are approaching. It is
  a constant-rate estimate, not a prediction that collision will occur.
  Pack-level = trial minimum; timeline = value at that stamp.
- `peak_t`: closest approach or collision time.
- `relevance_t`: the named conflict vehicle enters the conflict window.
- `brake_t`: onset of ego hard brake / sustained decelerate from the action log.
- **Center-to-center distance closing rate** (legacy JSON: `closing`):
  **−d(center-to-center distance)/dt** (m/s).
  Sign: `>0` ⇒ gap shrinking (approaching); `<0` ⇒ gap opening; ≈0 ⇒ steady.
  Context phrases this as `gap closing at X m/s` / `gap opening at X m/s`.
  This is **not** `ego speed − named vehicle speed`. To judge who is catching whom,
  compare both absolute speeds together with this distance trend.
- `impact` (present only when outcome = collision): rule-based collision geometry at
  the COLLISION frame — named vehicle azimuth relative to Ego heading, both vehicle
  speeds, and center-to-center distance closing rate,
  `impact_type` (`rear-end` or `side/lateral impact`), and for a rear-end,
  `striking_vehicle` (closed the gap, hit) / `struck_vehicle` (rear absorbed it) —
  both null for a side/lateral impact. Ground truth — quote it directly
  (e.g. "CuttingIn strikes Ego's rear"); do not re-derive or contradict from
  center-to-center distance / azimuth.
- **Named vehicle position in Ego coordinates** (legacy JSON: `rel_long_m` /
  `rel_lat_m`): named vehicle **center** relative to Ego **center**, after rotating
  world axes so Ego's heading is +forward and Ego's left is +left. Context writes
  directions explicitly, e.g. `(9.3 m behind ego, 1.5 m left of ego)`. This is
  center-to-center geometry, not bumper/nose tip.
- **Minimum distance between vehicle boundaries** (legacy JSON:
  `clearance_m`): shortest Euclidean distance between the boundaries of the two
  oriented vehicle rectangles (m). This is a vehicle-geometry term, unrelated
  to PCB or electrical clearance. It is zero when the rectangles touch or overlap.
  This distance quantifies geometric proximity; it does **not** decide whether
  the trial is a collision. Collision / safe outcome comes from ground truth
  (`collided`, Payload `collisionWith*`, or a COLLISION stamp in context).
- **Longitudinal relationship** (legacy JSON: `pass_state`): discrete bin of the
  longitudinal center offset (named vehicle center ahead/behind Ego center along Ego
  heading), with a ±2.5 m band (~half vehicle length). Context phrases:
  - `named vehicle ahead of Ego` — named vehicle center > 2.5 m ahead of Ego center
  - `vehicle lengths overlap longitudinally` — |ahead/behind offset| ≤ 2.5 m
    (centers roughly abreast; lateral gap may still be large)
  - `named vehicle behind Ego (Ego has passed)` — named vehicle center > 2.5 m behind Ego
  A transition from ahead → behind establishes that Ego passed the named vehicle.
  Do not confuse this with minimum distance between vehicle boundaries.
- **Ego average speed during the previous 1 second** (legacy JSON:
  `rolling_speed_1s_mps`) and **Ego distance traveled during the previous
  1 second** (legacy JSON: `rolling_disp_1s_m`): sustained Ego motion.
  Prefer these over one instantaneous Ego-speed sample.
  Do **not** invent short tokens like `rel=`, `roll1s=`, `pass=`, `az=`, or `d=`.
- Conflict pack window (BEV / context stamps): default **15 s before** peak through
  **8 s after** peak (asymmetric; captures pre-conflict setup). Legacy ±W if set.

**BEV reading guide (how frames are drawn)**
- Layout:
  - Medoid: **single panel**, Ego-centered. Half-extent = Ego–named vehicle center
    distance + 2 m (clamped ~8–40 m, expanded to fit vehicle footprints).
    Fixed Replayer orientation: rotate once from the trial's **initial** ego yaw
    so that heading points up, then only recenter — do not re-rotate per frame.
  - Parameter-space synced pair: dual panel — left = cluster A, right = cluster B
    at the **same shared clock** `t` (StartValidCondition clip per trial).
    Both panels share one scale: the larger center-to-center distance + 2 m.
    Each panel uses
    that trial's own fixed initial-yaw rotation.
- Title / filename event token (same rule everywhere): includes burst timing when
  present, e.g. `0p5s_before_APPROACH_Oncoming`, `0p5s_after_POST_Oncoming`,
  `COLLISION_Oncoming`. Context lines use the same timing in words
  (“0.5s before approach phase…”). Caption `±Xm` is the camera half-extent.
- Agents: Ego = orange box; other vehicles = blue. On-car ID circle = legend ID.
- Velocity arrows (from vehicle nose): **length ∝ speed**; Ego = dark green;
  others = cyan/blue. No arrow ≈ stationary / near-zero speed.
- Past path (trail up to this frame): Ego = orange line; others = blue line.
- Overlays: top-left legend (ID → role + speed); bottom-left scale bar (m);
  bottom-right center-to-center distance / estimated time-to-collision chip on
  conflict/pair panels when available;
  red road-ID / black lane-ID boxes on highlighted corridor roads.
- Prefer metrics + context timeline if a BEV overlay is ambiguous.

**Geometry (canonical nouns — defined once)**
- **Named vehicle azimuth relative to Ego heading**: bearing to the named vehicle, in degrees.
  Computed as direction-to-named-vehicle minus Ego nose heading, wrapped to (−180, 180].
  - 0° = straight ahead
  - + = named vehicle on Ego's left
  - − = named vehicle on Ego's right
  - ±180° = directly behind
  Context writes `CuttingIn azimuth relative to Ego heading=BEHIND LEFT (129°)` —
  read both; do not invent a
  separate “sector” metric. Do not use short tokens like `az=`.
  Label bins (same angle, for reading only): FRONT |azimuth|≤30°; FRONT LEFT 30–60°;
  LEFT 60–120°; BEHIND LEFT 120–150°; mirrored on the right; else BEHIND.
- **`Δazimuth`**: change in azimuth over time (e.g. FRONT→LEFT, or +20°). Same coordinate.
- Distance bands (from center-to-center distance): far ≥40 m, moderate 15–40, near 5–15,
  very close <5.
- Named vehicle speed and center-distance trend appear in timelines once distance is
  near/very-close (<15 m) or at COLLISION — compare against Ego speed to determine
  who is catching whom.
- **Δheading**: change in nose yaw (°); + ≈ left (CCW), − ≈ right (CW).
- **heading vs road direction** (`heading_vs_road`, JSON `vs_road`): nose heading
  minus local lane/road tangent; 0° = nose parallel to road. `+heading_vs_road` =
  nose to road-left; `-heading_vs_road` = nose to road-right. Not the same as
  azimuth to another agent.
  Do NOT infer severity from angle alone — use angle and speed:
  - lateral speed ≈ Ego speed × sin(|heading relative to road|) (m/s)
  - low lateral effect: `lateral_speed < 0.3 m/s`
  - moderate: `0.3–0.8 m/s`
  - strong: `> 0.8 m/s`

**Rule-based actions (trust the log; do not invent)**
- Speed: ACCELERATE / DECELERATE / EMERGENCY_BRAKE / STOPPED.
- Lane: LANE_CHANGE_LEFT / RIGHT (lane_id change).
- Same-lane TURN_LEFT / RIGHT: heading change without lane_id change (≥15°, ≥1.5 s).
- Junction ENTER/EXIT: separate; turn intent there uses a larger junction threshold.
- COLLISION / NEAR_MISS: the named vehicle appears in the log (`with_name`).
- APPROACH_* / POST_*: conflict burst samples around peak (often labeled
  `Ns before/after` in context and BEV titles).

**Motive codes (CLOSED set — single-trial ego behavior only)**
Pick from this list; do not invent new codes or free-form motive names. Each code
needs at least one cited metric (peak time / center-to-center distance /
estimated time to collision / brake onset) or one context/BEV stamp (`t=…`,
frame name).

If the supplied context already states pass/yield (e.g. a pair pack's
Deterministic interaction geometry block), trust that unless it contradicts
the control arc below. Otherwise infer it from **longitudinal relationship
AND brake/accelerate**. A late brake into overlap is a failed yield, not a
pass. Braking is not yield only when Ego then continues through (named
vehicle behind, or brake_release then continues).

| code | applies when |
| ---- | ------------ |
| `yield_to_vehicle` | Ego decelerates / stops to give way: named vehicle stays ahead through the outcome, **or** Ego is still braking when a collision/overlap cuts the tape (failed yield). Braking alone is NOT yield if Ego already passed (`named vehicle behind Ego`) or released the brake and continued. FRONT→LEFT overlap while still decelerating stays this family, not `pass_first`. |
| `early_brake` | sustained deceleration begins while estimated time to collision > 3.5 s or the named vehicle is outside the immediate conflict zone; yield-family, including a failed yield that still collides in overlap |
| `late_reaction` | first strong deceleration / EMERGENCY_BRAKE only near peak conflict (or estimated time to collision < 1.5 s); yield-family, including a failed yield that still collides in overlap while Ego is braking. Do not switch to `assertive_gap_acceptance` just because overlap or collision follows a late brake. |
| `gap_acceptance_creep` | ego keeps a low non-zero speed into the conflict instead of stopping |
| `assertive_gap_acceptance` | ego holds or increases speed into a closing gap before peak conflict, no brake |
| `maintain_through` | \|Δego speed\| ≲ 2 m/s across the conflict window; no brake stamp (smooth pass when Ego also passes) |
| `post_clear_recovery` | accelerate or same-lane turn **after** peak conflict, named vehicle already behind |
| `post_clear_hard_brake` | severe/abrupt decelerate (EMERGENCY_BRAKE or \|Δv\| ≳5 m/s) strictly **after** peak conflict, named vehicle already behind/clear — Ego brakes hard for a new reason (e.g. another obstacle), not because the just-passed vehicle is re-closing; this creates rear-end risk for that vehicle and is the direct precursor to a Brake Rear-end collision |
| `vehicle_driven_swerve` | same-lane TURN_* inside the conflict window with moderate+ lateral effect |
| `brake_release` | Ego ENDS/eases an active decelerate (that itself was `yield_to_vehicle` / `late_reaction` / `early_brake`) **before** peak/outcome, while estimated time to collision ≲ 3 s or the named vehicle is still near/very-close — braking stops before the conflict is resolved |
| `post_clear_adjustment` | light/moderate decelerate (or a second short accelerate) strictly **after** peak conflict, while trailing a named vehicle that is NOT re-closing (center-to-center flat/opening, estimated time to collision not tightening) — a following-distance correction, not a new evasive response and not the resuming-speed story of `post_clear_recovery` |
| `unclear` | metrics and BEV genuinely disagree (e.g. contradictory log text) or evidence is missing → say so in `open_questions`. Do NOT use this just because an action doesn't feel "assertive" or "recovery" — check `brake_release`, `post_clear_hard_brake`, and `post_clear_adjustment` first (see precedence below) |

**Precedence for brake-release / post-peak actions (check BEFORE defaulting to `unclear`)**
1. Ego ends/eases an active evasive decelerate, named vehicle still near/very-close or `ttc` still tight, stamp is before `peak_t`/outcome ⇒ `brake_release` (this is often the direct precursor to a collision — name it, don't bury it in `unclear`).
2. Stamp is after `peak_t`, action is a severe/abrupt decelerate (EMERGENCY_BRAKE or |Δv| ≳5 m/s) unrelated to the just-passed named vehicle re-closing ⇒ `post_clear_hard_brake` (the direct precursor to a Brake Rear-end collision — name it, don't bury it in `unclear`).
3. Stamp is after `peak_t`, action is a minor decelerate/accelerate, named vehicle not re-closing ⇒ `post_clear_adjustment`.
4. Stamp is after `peak_t`, action is accelerate or same-lane turn, named vehicle already clear/behind ⇒ `post_clear_recovery` (unchanged).
5. Only if none of the above apply AND the evidence itself is contradictory/insufficient ⇒ `unclear`.

**Interaction resolution vs control (factor together — then motive)**
Decide **who gets priority** from geometry AND the conflict control arc
(brake / accelerate / hold / brake_release). If context already prints a
pass/yield verdict, use it unless it contradicts that control arc.

`interaction_resolution` (closed):
- `yield` — Ego decelerates/stops to give way and the named vehicle is **not**
  behind Ego. Includes completed stay-ahead yield **and** failed yield:
  FRONT→LEFT overlap or collision while Ego is still braking (too late / too
  weak). That overlap does **not** become `pass_first`. Covers `proactive-yield`,
  `late-yield`, `yield`, `yield-stop-collision`.
- `pass_first` — Ego proceeds through. ANY of these (azimuth BEHIND is **not**
  required):
  - longitudinal relationship becomes `named vehicle behind Ego (Ego has passed)`
    (the behind bin, not the overlap band);
  - Ego holds or increases speed into the closing gap (no yield brake);
  - `brake_release` then Ego continues through;
  - azimuth FRONT→LEFT→BEHIND while Ego continues.
  Includes `pass-first`, `smooth-pass`, `pass-slowdown`, post-pass
  `braking-rear-end`, and a failed pass **after** Ego already went through.
- `unresolved` — conflict ends without a clear pass or yield (no behind, no
  give-way brake, no hold/accel through).

`interaction_resolution=yield` remains `YIELD` whether Ego brakes early, brakes
late, stops, or the brake is insufficient and the trial collides in overlap.
Use `control_response` for timing/quality. Do not flip a still-braking overlap
collision to `pass_first`. Do not infer yield from braking if Ego already passed.

`control_response` (closed; how Ego manages speed — not who went first):
- `smooth` / `maintain` — little or no brake through/after pass (`smooth-pass`)
- `slowdown` — gradual decelerate (`pass-slowdown`)
- `proactive` — brake while the named vehicle is still far / early (`proactive-yield`)
- `late` — brake only near peak (`late-yield`)
- `stop` — decelerate to near-zero (`yield-stop-collision` when colliding)
- `brake` — severe/abrupt brake, often post-pass (`braking-rear-end`)
- `recovery` — post-clear accelerate / heading recover
- `none` — no distinctive control

**Filling `interaction_resolution` / `control_response` / `primary_motive` / `secondary_motives` together**
These four fields answer different questions and must be filled in this order,
each constrained by the one before it:
1. `interaction_resolution` — WHO kept priority, from geometry AND control
   (give-way decelerate vs go-through). Overlap + still braking = failed
   `yield`. Named vehicle behind, or hold/accel / brake_release then continue =
   `pass_first`. Braking never decides this by itself if Ego already passed.
2. `control_response` — HOW Ego managed speed while (1) happened (a manner
   bucket, not a cause).
3. `primary_motive` — WHY, the single dominant closed motive code that
   causally explains the arc, consistent with (1) and (2).
4. `secondary_motives` — every other distinct non-null closed code used on
   the timeline besides `primary_motive` (0-2 codes, prioritize ones tied to
   the peak/outcome). Leave `[]` only when one code truly covers the whole arc.

**`control_response` must agree with the motive that produced it**
Each `control_response` value only pairs with the motive codes below. If the
timeline's dominant motive and `control_response` would disagree, trust the
motive evidence and correct `control_response` — never leave a contradiction:

| `control_response` | only pairs with |
| ---- | ---- |
| `proactive` | `early_brake` |
| `late` | `late_reaction`, `brake_release` |
| `stop` | `yield_to_vehicle`, or a collision-frame stamp |
| `brake` | `post_clear_hard_brake` (severe/abrupt, post-pass) |
| `smooth` / `maintain` | `maintain_through`, `assertive_gap_acceptance` |
| `slowdown` | `assertive_gap_acceptance` (gradual, still passing), `gap_acceptance_creep`, `post_clear_adjustment` |
| `recovery` | `post_clear_recovery` |
| `none` | `unclear`, `vehicle_driven_swerve`, or no brake stamp on the timeline |

This also settles the `yield_to_vehicle` vs `early_brake` vs `late_reaction`
choice: since `proactive` pairs ONLY with `early_brake` and `late` pairs ONLY
with `late_reaction`/`brake_release`, `yield_to_vehicle` can never be
`primary_motive` when `control_response` is `proactive` or `late` — pick
`early_brake`/`late_reaction` instead, they are more specific (they also name
WHEN the yield committed). Reserve `yield_to_vehicle` as `primary_motive` for
`control_response=stop`. Use `gap_acceptance_creep` (pairs with `slowdown`)
when Ego keeps a low non-zero speed instead of stopping. The code you did not
pick may still appear in `secondary_motives`.

**Evidence hierarchy (do not invert)**
1. Ground-truth outcome and minimum distance between vehicle boundaries.
2. Longitudinal relationship / named vehicle position in Ego coordinates /
   relative-angle FRONT→BEHIND.
3. Ego distance traveled and average speed during the previous 1 second.
4. Instantaneous Ego speed / action stamps.
A single velocity sample or sub-second brake-onset difference is **not causal**
without persistent geometric separation. Oscillatory Δv is noise.

**Resolution class (derived, closed set — used to build the cluster_summary `label`)**
Prefer the trial's `interaction_resolution` field when present. Otherwise map
motive codes — but never call YIELD merely because ego braked:

| source | resolution class |
| ---- | ---- |
| `interaction_resolution=pass_first` | PASS |
| `interaction_resolution=yield` | YIELD |
| `assertive_gap_acceptance` / `maintain_through` / `post_clear_recovery` | PASS |
| `yield_to_vehicle` (only if Ego remains behind) | YIELD |
| `early_brake` + still behind | YIELD |
| `gap_acceptance_creep` | YIELD (creeping) |
| `late_reaction` | timing modifier only — combine with resolution |
| `brake_release` | response event only — combine with resolution |
| `post_clear_hard_brake` | PASS (risk modifier — pairs with the `Brake Rear-end` label) |
| `vehicle_driven_swerve` | OTHER (lateral resolution) |
| `unclear` / `unresolved` | OTHER |

**Cluster label building blocks (cluster_summary `label` ONLY — see cluster_summary_prompt)**
`label` is composed, not freehanded, from closed blocks:
`[Resolution][ Modifier][ Collision|Near-miss]` — 2-4 words, Title Case, hyphens allowed.

- Resolution (required): `Pass-First` | `Pass` | `Yield` | `<Named vehicle> Collision`
  (`Pass-First` when `interaction_resolution=pass_first`; `Yield` when yield;
  named vehicle name instead of Pass/Yield ONLY when resolution is OTHER/unresolved
  and the cluster is collision-dominant, e.g. "CuttingIn Collision",
  "Oncoming Collision", "Parking Collision").
- Modifier (optional, at most one, from `control_response` / timeline intensity):
  `Smooth` (`smooth`/`maintain`, safe pass) | `Slowdown` (gradual brake, still
  pass) | `Proactive` (early brake while yielding) | `Late` (brake near peak) |
  `Creep` (`gap_acceptance_creep`) | `Stop` (decelerates to near-zero) |
  `Brake` (severe/abrupt, often post-pass rear-end — driven by the medoid's
  `post_clear_hard_brake` primary/secondary motive, not freehanded).
- Outcome suffix: `Collision` when collision_rate is the majority (or medoid is
  collision with no contradicting pair evidence). `Near-miss` when near_miss
  dominates. Omit when mostly `safe`. For rear-end after a pass use
  `Brake Rear-end` (modifier Brake + implied rear-end collision), not Yield.

Examples (allowed naming density — vocabulary only, not literal facts):
"Pass-First" · "Smooth Pass" · "Pass-Slowdown" · "Yield" · "Proactive Yield" ·
"Late Yield" · "Yield-Stop Collision" · "Brake Rear-end" · "Cut-in Collision" ·
"Oncoming Collision" · "Parking Collision".
Do not add adjectives outside these blocks (no "assertive", "aggressive", "gentle", …
inside `label`) — richer color belongs in `caption`, not `label`.

**Causal locality (no future evidence)**
- An ego action at time `t_a` may be explained only by evidence at `t ≤ t_a`, plus
  global ground truth (`peak_t`, `d`, `ttc`, outcome).
-   Later events may appear in the timeline, but must NOT be given as the reason for an
  earlier action. Post-peak accelerate / same-lane turn while the named vehicle stays clear
  ⇒ `post_clear_recovery`; post-peak light/moderate decelerate while the named vehicle stays
  clear (not re-closing) ⇒ `post_clear_adjustment`; post-peak severe/abrupt decelerate
  unrelated to the passed vehicle re-closing ⇒ `post_clear_hard_brake`. None of these is
  ever the motive for a pre-peak brake.
- Stamps where the named vehicle is still far (d ≳ 100 m) are scene setup, not conflict
  motives, unless a metric ties them to the conflict.

**Wording**
- Longitudinal: "slow down" / "speed up" / "hard brake" / "maintain speed" +
  lightly|moderately|severely — never "speed adjustment".
- Same-lane: "turn left/right (same lane)"; quote Δheading; not a lane change
  unless LANE_CHANGE_* is present. Ignore junction enter/exit filler for motives.

**Honesty**
- One medoid ≠ whole cluster. IC percentiles are descriptive, not safe/risky bounds.
- If BEV and metrics disagree, prefer metrics and note uncertainty.
