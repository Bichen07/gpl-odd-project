<Domain Rules & Glossary>

Do not recompute a number the context already states. Do not invent abbreviations.

**Center-to-center distance.** Straight-line distance between the two vehicle centers (m). On a timeline line it is the value at that stamp. In pack-level conflict metrics it is the closest approach of the trial.

**Minimum distance between vehicle boundaries.** Shortest distance between the two vehicle rectangles (m). Zero when they touch or overlap. This length is not the center distance, and it does not decide collision. Collision or safe comes from the recorded outcome or a COLLISION stamp.

**Ego speed.** Ego's ground speed at that stamp (m/s). Named-vehicle speed is that vehicle's ground speed, not a relative speed.

**Closing rate.** How fast the center distance is shrinking (m/s). Positive means the gap is shrinking; negative means it is opening. Context writes `closing at` or `opening at`. This is not Ego speed minus the other vehicle's speed. To see who is catching whom, read both speeds together with this rate.

**Estimated time to collision.** Center distance divided by the closing rate while the vehicles approach. A constant-rate estimate, not a prediction that a collision will occur. On a timeline line it is the value at that stamp; in pack-level metrics it is the trial minimum.

**Position in Ego coordinates.** The named vehicle's center relative to Ego's center, with Ego's heading as forward and Ego's left as left. Example: `(9.3 m behind ego, 1.5 m left of ego)`. Centers, not bumpers.

**Longitudinal relationship.** A bin of that forward offset, cut at ±2.5 m:
- ahead of Ego — center more than 2.5 m forward
- vehicle lengths overlap longitudinally — the forward offset is within 2.5 m (the lateral gap may still be large)
- behind Ego (Ego has passed) — center more than 2.5 m back
Ahead then behind means Ego passed. Do not confuse this bin with the body gap.

**Azimuth relative to Ego heading.** Bearing to the named vehicle, degrees, wrapped to (−180, 180]. 0° is straight ahead, positive is Ego's left, negative is Ego's right, ±180° is behind. Context writes the sector and the degrees together (`BEHIND LEFT (129°)`). Bins, for reading the same angle: FRONT within 30°; FRONT LEFT 30–60°; LEFT 60–120°; BEHIND LEFT 120–150°; mirrored on the right; else BEHIND.

**Heading vs road.** Nose heading minus the local road tangent. 0° means the nose is parallel to the road. Positive is nose to road-left. This is not the azimuth to another vehicle. Do not read severity from the angle alone; the line also gives `lateral_speed`.

**Δheading.** Change in nose yaw. Positive is left, negative is right.

**Times in the pack.** `peak_t` is closest approach or collision. `relevance_t` is when the named vehicle enters the conflict window. `brake_t` is the onset of the hard brake or the sustained decelerate in the action log. Stamps run from 15 s before the peak through 8 s after it.

**Impact** (only when the outcome is collision). Judge it at the COLLISION stamp from the position line, both speeds, the closing rate, and the image. Quote azimuth and both speeds.
- `striking_vehicle` is the vehicle whose body moves into the other; `struck_vehicle` is the one that is hit. A vehicle under about 0.3 m/s while the other moves is struck. When both move and the gap is closing, the vehicle behind is striking. When the gap is opening, the vehicle ahead is moving away, and the contact comes from a turn or a swinging corner: name that vehicle by its motion.
- When the evidence leaves it open, write null for both vehicles and give the reason in `open_questions`.
- Name Ego's touching corner from the position line alone: other vehicle ahead and left of Ego is Ego's front-left; ahead and right is front-right; behind and left is rear-left; behind and right is rear-right. `left of ego` is Ego's left.
- Name only the part of the other vehicle that faces Ego: its rear when it is ahead of Ego, its front when it is behind Ego, its side when it is beside Ego. Leave out its left and right. Example: CuttingIn ahead and left of Ego gives "CuttingIn's rear meets Ego's front-left."
- `impact_type` follows the two heading arrows on the collision image. The context line has no heading for the other vehicle, so read the arrows. A touch at a box corner does not decide the type: two boxes almost always meet at a corner unless they overlap on one line.
  - `rear-end`: the arrows point nearly the same way, the gap is closing, and the vehicle behind drives into the one ahead. A corner of the box touching is still a rear-end.
  - `head-on`: the arrows point nearly opposite ways, and the two fronts meet.
  - `side-swipe`: the arrows differ by a large angle, so one front meets the other's side, or the vehicles run side by side and touch along a side. A corner of the box touching is still a side-swipe.
  - `corner contact`: one vehicle swings a corner into the other. Use it when the arrows point nearly the same way but the gap is opening or the vehicle behind is nearly stopped, because the vehicle ahead turned or changed lane into it. Use it also when the arrows are neither nearly parallel nor a clear side meeting.
  - `unclear`: the arrows, the speeds, and the position line do not settle it.
- `bearing_sector` is the azimuth bin, filled by the pipeline.

**BEV.** A medoid frame is one panel, Ego-centered, cropped to the named vehicle's center distance plus 2 m, heading up from the trial's initial yaw. That crop follows the named vehicle. A pair frame is two panels at the same clock, one scale (the larger center distance plus 2 m). If the image and the text disagree, use the text and say so in `open_questions`.

**Stamp reading.**
- A secondary-agent clause (name, speed, clearance, position) is a fact, not a pre-assigned cause.
- Ego eases a decelerate while the named vehicle is still close, before the peak: the why says the brake ended early.
- After an agent is already behind and nobody else is ahead, an accelerate or a turn has no why.

**Resolution (factor this, nothing else)**
`resolution` is go-through vs give-way **vs that agent**. Decide it from geometry
AND the conflict control arc (brake / accelerate / hold / brake_release). If
context already prints a pass/yield verdict, use it unless it contradicts that
control arc.

`resolution` (closed):
- `yield` — Ego decelerates/stops to give way and the named vehicle is **not**
  behind Ego. Includes completed stay-ahead yield **and** failed yield:
  FRONT→LEFT overlap or collision while Ego is still braking (too late / too
  weak). That overlap does **not** become `pass_first`. Early or late, the
  resolution is still `yield`.
- `pass_first` — Ego proceeds through. ANY of these (azimuth BEHIND is **not**
  required):
  - longitudinal relationship becomes `named vehicle behind Ego (Ego has passed)`
    (the behind bin, not the overlap band);
  - Ego holds or increases speed into the closing gap (no yield brake);
  - `brake_release` then Ego continues through;
  - azimuth FRONT→LEFT→BEHIND while Ego continues.
  Includes a failed pass **after** Ego already went through.
- `unresolved` — conflict ends without a clear pass or yield (no behind, no
  give-way brake, no hold/accel through).

`resolution=yield` remains `YIELD` whether Ego brakes early, brakes
late, stops, or the brake is insufficient and the trial collides in overlap.
Do not flip a still-braking overlap collision to `pass_first`. Do not infer
yield from braking if Ego already passed. A name such as `Late Yield` belongs
on `cluster_summary.label`, not on this row.

**Arc window.** Each `agent_interactions` row uses only the stamps concluded toward that agent, from first engagement until that agent's pass, yield, or unresolved is decided. One row per agent Ego responded to. Named vehicle first. Each row is `agent` + `resolution` only. Later stamps toward another agent do not change this row.

Example: Ego holds speed through CuttingIn (`pass_first`), then decelerates with Parking ahead. CuttingIn stays `pass_first`. The later brake is Parking's row.

Resume driving, with the last conflict agent already behind and nobody ahead, is not a resolution. Omit the why.

**Evidence order.** Do not invert this:
1. Recorded outcome and minimum distance between vehicle boundaries.
2. Longitudinal relationship, position in Ego coordinates, and FRONT→BEHIND.
3. Ego speed at that stamp.
4. Action stamps.
One speed sample, or a sub-second brake-onset gap, is not a cause unless the geometry stays split. An action at time t may be explained only with evidence at or before t, plus the recorded outcome. Later events may appear in the timeline and must not be given as the reason for an earlier action. A stamp while the named vehicle is still beyond about 100 m is setup, not the conflict why, unless a metric ties it to the conflict.

**Honesty.** One medoid is not the whole cluster. If the image and the metrics disagree, prefer the metrics and note the uncertainty.

</Domain Rules & Glossary>
