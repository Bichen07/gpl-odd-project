<Your Task>
Write a CLUSTER behavior summary for the WHOLE cluster (not one medoid journey)
AND describe how its trajectory differs from its parameter-space-matched neighbors.

Primary inputs (required upstream):
1. This cluster's `output/medoid_trial.yaml` — archetype behavior.
2. Parameter-space pair contrast files that touch this cluster (`parameter_space_pairs/c*-c*/output/contrast.yaml`)

3. `processed/context_cluster.md` — the collision count only: collisions/trials (rate%).

**You invent `label`.** 2–4 words that say what this cluster did. One mechanism. The why belongs in `caption`.

Use plain nouns and no adjective (not late, proactive, smooth, hard). You do not see the other clusters, so a degree word has nothing to compare against. A later review pass adds those words when two clusters share a name.

No collision: name the outcome, for example `Pass`, `Yield`, `Stop`, `Slowdown`.

Collision: read the `collision` line of the medoid card (impact_type, striking_vehicle, struck_vehicle). Name the impact type and the two vehicles, striking first, for example `CuttingIn Rear-end Ego`. If the card has no `collision` line, name the impact from the timeline and say so in `caption`.

Do not chain two mechanisms into one name.

**Cluster provenance:** MFPCA followed by HDBSCAN was run on the **whole
trajectory**. A cluster therefore groups trajectory shape and termination, not
necessarily one interaction intent. At a collision/behavior boundary, one
parameter-space edge may be a pass attempt that collides while another is a
yield attempt that also collides, even when their trajectories look similar.
Do not force those boundary cases into the medoid's resolution; use the
pattern of pair readings to describe the mix.

Describe what this cluster shares with a neighbor and what still differs. Do not recommend a merge, a split, or a different number of clusters.
