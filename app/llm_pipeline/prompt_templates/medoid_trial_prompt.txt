<Motive summary guide>
Write why Ego behaves this way across the trial. It is not a recap of
`decision_timeline`.

Write **2–4 short sentences** in one paragraph. Hard cap ~80 words.
If CuttingIn then Parking both matter, two sentences for CuttingIn, one for Parking.

Each sentence answers why, with at most **one** quoted anchor (time or distance or
speed) — not a WHEN/WHAT/SITUATION/LINK template.

Rules:
- Plain English. A cluster name such as `Late Yield` is written later by
  cluster_summary, not here.
- State pass vs yield vs the **named vehicle** once, in words (“Ego goes first”
  / “Ego gives way”).
- Do not re-walk every stamp. The timeline already lists the actions.
- Do not invent actions absent from context/BEV.
- Collision / near-miss: one sentence with the peak numbers that matter
  (who, side, min distance or speeds) — not a second timeline.

BAD (too long, stamp recap):
  Ego initially holds speed, then at 8.1 s the gap is 20 m, then at 9.4 s it
  brakes, then at 10.8 s the gap is 5 m, then at 12 s it accelerates again …

GOOD:
  Ego goes first: it keeps speeding up while CuttingIn is still ahead and the
  gap shrinks to ~9 m, then brakes only when CuttingIn is already on the left
  (~5 m) — late, but it still misses (min boundary 1.4 m) and passes. After
  CuttingIn is behind, Ego slows again because Parking is ahead and closing.
